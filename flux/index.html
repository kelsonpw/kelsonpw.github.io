BEHIND THE FIELD

Simple rules.
Complex beauty.

A damped wave simulation on a coupled grid. Each point responds to its neighbors. Disturbances propagate, reflect at the edges, and interfere. Lime-to-white highlights show local motion and slope.

Tap to pluck. Hold to drive a source, or enable two sources and adjust their frequency. Select Orbit to turn the camera. On the canvas, arrows orbit, +/− zoom, R sends a ripple, and Space pauses. Reduced motion starts paused; Send ripple advances a still preview. Share setup saves the camera and controls, not the exact history of your ripples.

An experiment by Kelson Warner. Runs entirely in your browser. The other forms are mathematical sculptures, not physical simulations.
